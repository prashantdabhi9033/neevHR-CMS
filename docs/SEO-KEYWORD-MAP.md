# NeevHR — Master SEO Keyword & Search Intent Map

This document establishes the strategic keyword-to-URL ownership architecture for **https://www.neevhr.com**.

## Purpose & Strategy
- **One Primary Intent = One Primary Page:** Strict ownership prevents internal keyword cannibalization.
- **India-First Statutory Focus:** Captures high-intent commercial buyers searching for statutory compliance (PF, ESI, PT, LWF, TDS).
- **Topical Authority Clusters:** Educational guides, statutory calculators, and glossary definitions channel authority directly into the core commercial pillar pages.
